Software

watchengine + batwatch

Project details

watchengine ↗   batwatch ↗

Why I built them
I wanted a way to check whether a watch or car listing was actually priced well. I built two Python tools to collect listings, compare prices, and narrow down which ones were worth a closer look.
How they work
watchengine compares watch listings with prices for the same reference. batwatch looks at Bring a Trailer auctions and estimates the room left after purchase fees, tax, transport, and other costs. Both keep data in SQLite so I can compare listings across runs.
The hard part
The comparison only helps if the underlying items are similar. The tools track comparable sales and use minimum sample requirements. A flag is a reason to investigate a listing, and I am still checking the live results.
